# M33 — Item Pool Expansion Part 1

**Status:** 🚧 in progress
**Depends on:** M31

---

## Goal

Introduce "One-Bit" unique Tier 2 equipment (Uncommon/Rare) and specialized consumables with a focus on elemental variety and class utility.

---

## Scope

- **High-Aesthetic Selection**: Masterwork and elemental-infused weapons with a "digital/glitch" aesthetic (e.g. *Bit-Stream Rapier*, *Static-Core Mace*).
- **Elemental Spread**: Items covering Fire, Frost, Lightning, and Poison (e.g. *Ring of Fire Resistance*, *Lightning Spear*).
- **Class-Specific Gear**: 
    - **Fighter**: Half-plate of Grounding, Vicious Battleaxe.
    - **Wizard**: Chronos-Woven Robes, Scroll of Misty Step.
    - **Rogue**: Shadow-Step Boots, Poisoned Dagger.
- **Utility Items**: Antivenoms, Elixir of Heroism, Amulet of Health.
- **Integration**: Distributed across regional chests and tied as rewards for the M34 quest chains.

---

## Target Files

- `assets/items/`: Define new TOML files for each item.
- `src/app/samples.rs`: Update sample pools if necessary.

---

## Done When

- [x] 20+ "One-Bit" unique items properly serialize/deserialize.
- [x] Items display correctly in Inventory UI with thematic descriptions.
- [ ] Tests verify elemental damage/damage resistance mechanics are functioning.
- [x] Loot distribution across at least 3 regions (Valley, Woods, Summit).
