# M33 — Item Pool Expansion Part 1

**Status:** ✅ completed
**Depends on:** M31

---

## Goal

Introduce Tier 2 equipment (Uncommon/Rare) and specialized consumables to support mid-level scaling.

---

## Scope

- **Weaponry**: Masterwork, Silvered, or Elemental-infused basic weapons.
- **Armor**: Scale mail, Half-plate, enchanted robes.
- **Consumables**: Invisibility potions, antivenoms, scroll of misty step.
- **Loot Tables**: Disperse these items to region-specific chests or enemy drop tables.

---

## Target Files

- `assets/items.toml`: Defining properties and bonuses.
- `assets/spells.toml`: Linking scrolls to mechanics.

---

## Done When

- [x] 20+ new items properly serialize/deserialize.
- [x] Items display correctly in Inventory UI.
- [x] Tests verify elemental damage/damage resistance mechanics on specialized armors/weapons.
