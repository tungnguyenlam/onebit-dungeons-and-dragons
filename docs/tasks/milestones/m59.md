# Milestone 59 — Dynamic Weather & Environmental Hazards

**Goal:** Make the world feel alive and dangerous by implementing weather patterns and tactical hazards on the room grid.

**Status:** ✅ COMPLETED

**Design:**
1. **Weather System:** Implement `WeatherType` (Rain, Ash, Fog, Snow) that changes based on region and time. 
   - *Rain:* Disadvantage on Fire-based spells, -2 on ranged attacks.
   - *Fog:* Reduced FOV radius.
   - *Ash:* Periodic Con save or be "Coughing" (disadvantage on next action).
2. **Grid Hazards:** Add `Hazard` property to tiles (e.g., Lava, Deep Water, Ice, Spikes).
3. **Tactical Interaction:** Allow spells to interact with hazards (e.g., Fireball turns Ice into Water, Ray of Frost turns Water into Ice).

**Files to create/modify:**
- `src/game/world/mod.rs` (weather state logic)
- `src/game/world/tile.rs` (hazard effects)
- `src/ui/tui/screens/world_map.rs` (visual indicators for weather)
- `src/game/combat/attack.rs` (weather-based penalties)

**Done when:**
- [x] Regions display their current weather effects in the TUI.
- [x] Player takes damage or receives conditions when stepping into hazard tiles.
- [x] Ranged combat penalties apply during Fog/Rain.
- [x] FOV radius correctly shrinks in Foggy conditions.
