# M34 — Multi-Region Quest Chains

**Status:** 🔲 planned
**Depends on:** M30

---

## Goal

Design multi-region quests centered around a grand Epic Threat (e.g., an awakening Ancient Red Dragon or Lich) that forces the player to hunt for critical artifacts (MacGuffins) across the Valley of Ash, Whispering Woods, and Sunken City.

---

## Scope

- **The Epic Threat**: Centralize the narrative around a singular, world-ending antagonist whose influence taints the regions.
- **MacGuffin Hunt**: Introduce 3 unique legendary artifacts needed to breach the antagonist's lair or weaken their defenses.
- **Cross-Region Flags**: Ensure `WorldState` manages progress as the player retrieves the artifacts and witnesses the antagonist's escalating influence.
- **Journal Organization**: Better categorize main scenario quests vs. region-specific side lines.

---

## Target Files

- `assets/quests.toml`: New epic quest branches.
- `assets/regions/**/dialog/`: Update NPCs in various hubs to reference the central struggle.

---

## Done When

- [ ] A continuous quest requires actions in at least 3 regions to complete.
- [ ] `validate_assets.sh` passes with the cross-referenced flags.
- [ ] All journal updates correctly trigger based on cross-region conditions.
