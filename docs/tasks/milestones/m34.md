# M34 — Multi-Region Quest Chains

**Status:** 🚧 in progress
**Depends on:** M30

---

## Goal

Design multi-region quests centered around an "Epic Threat" involving two primary antagonists: **Ignis the Cinder-Drake** and **Malphas the Void-Lich**. The player must hunt for legendary artifacts scattered across the world.

---

## Scope

- **Two Primary Antagonists**:
    - **Ignis the Cinder-Drake**: A rogue guardian of the Emberpeak Forge, corrupted by a "Hardware Glitch" (Dragon).
    - **Malphas the Void-Lich**: An ancient archmage whose soul was fragmented across the "Whispering Woods" data-stream (Lich).
- **The Scattered Legendaries**:
    - **The Obsidian Heart**: Held by Ignis in Emberpeak Summit.
    - **The Sylvan Glitch-Key**: Hidden in the deepest glade of the Whispering Woods.
    - **The Null-Scepter**: Fractured across the Sunken City.
- **Cross-Region Logic**: 
    - The quest *The Obsidian Scourge* will be refactored to bridge these regions.
    - WorldState flags will track the state of the "Glitch" influence in each hub.
- **Journal Organization**: High-priority "Epic" category for the main scenario quest line.

---

## Target Files

- `assets/quests/main/obsidian-scourge.toml`: Full refactor for the multi-boss flow.
- `assets/regions/**/dialog/`: Update NPCs to provide lore on the two threats.
- `src/game/story/world_state.rs`: Ensure flags support the scattered legendaries.

---
