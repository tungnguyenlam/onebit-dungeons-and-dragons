# M35 — Character Progression Level 5-10

**Status:** ✅ completed
**Depends on:** M33

---

## Goal

Extend the player progression curve beyond the introductory levels, introducing key tier-defining abilities like Extra Attack and 3rd/4th level spells.

---

## Scope

- **Class Scaling**: Update progression charts for Fighter, Wizard, and Rogue up to level 10.
- **Extra Attack**: Allow characters with the feature to make two attack rolls per `Attack` action.
- **Ability Score Improvements (ASI)**: Hook up stat increases at levels 4 and 8.
- **High-Tier Spells**: Fireball, Haste, Invisibility, Banishment.

---

## Target Files

- `src/game/character/progression.rs`: Update tables and ASI logic.
- `src/game/combat/`: Implement Extra Attack tracking in CombatState.
- `assets/spells.toml`: 3rd and 4th level spell definitions.

---

## Done When

- [x] Reaching level 5+ awards Extra Attack or 3rd level slots depending on class.
- [x] Extra attack functions natively in the combat loop.
- [x] At least 4 new high-tier spells are supported by `resolve_spell_effect()`.
