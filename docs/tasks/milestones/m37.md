# M37 — Bestiary & Item Pool Expansion Part 2

**Status:** 🔲 planned
**Depends on:** M36

---

## Goal

Populate the mid-late game regions (Sunken City and surrounding areas) with CR 6-8 threats and Tier 3 artifacts.

---

## Scope

- **Aquatic & Ruin Denizens**: Aboleths, Ghostly Knights, Merrow, and Water Elementals.
- **Magic Artifacts**: Items with powerful passive abilities (e.g., Ring of Free Action, Trident of Fish Command).
- **Condition Immunities**: Enemies immune to charm, poison, or non-magical weapons.

---

## Target Files

- `assets/monsters.toml`: CR 6-8 enemies.
- `assets/items.toml`: Legendary or highly rare equipment.

---

## Done When

- [ ] Enemies with immunities correctly shrug off applied conditions in combat.
- [ ] Aboleths and Water Elementals function cleanly in combat.
- [ ] Artifacts provide correct complex bonuses.
