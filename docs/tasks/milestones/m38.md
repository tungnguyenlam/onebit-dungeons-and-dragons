# M38 — Epic Threat Escalation

**Status:** 🔲 planned
**Depends on:** M34

---

## Goal

Escalate the Epic Threat introduced in M34. As the player gathers the MacGuffins, the antagonist begins aggressively altering the world map, proving they are an active, destructive force.

---

## Scope

- **World Events**: Town Guard vs. Antagonist's Minion encounters triggered when the epic quest advances.
- **Ruined Hubs**: Changing safe hubs into dangerous hazard zones based on WorldState flags (e.g., The antagonist attacks the Ironhold Mines).
- **Assassination Squads**: The antagonist sends tracking squads to ambush the player during overworld hub transitions.

---

## Target Files

- `src/game/story/events.rs`: Epic threat world mechanics.
- `assets/regions/` : Conditional rooms that open/close depending on WorldState flags representing the antagonist's influence.

---

## Done When

- [ ] Acquiring a MacGuffin alters the spawn states or hostile attitudes in previously safe hubs.
- [ ] High quest progress reliably spawns "tracker" combat encounters during transitions.
- [ ] Safe hubs change their layout or visual tags via conditional region loading.
